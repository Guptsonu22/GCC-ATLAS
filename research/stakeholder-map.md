## 2. Buyer vs user vs influencer (for segment A, the recommended primary — see opportunity-prioritization.md)

- **User:** the desk analyst / strategist at the expanding MNC who assembles the city shortlist.
- **Economic buyer:** head of corporate development / India initiative lead.
- **Influencers:** CBRE/JLL/Zinnov consultants already advising; parent-side real-estate leads.

## 3. What each stakeholder needs that GCC Atlas can serve (jobs-to-be-done)

- **A:** *"Compare feasible cities for a BFSI/AI hub by talent depth, salary bands, policy subsidies, real-estate cost, and peer presence — before talking to a broker."*
- **B:** *"Show global target companies that India's tier-2 / satellite-city models fit better than Bengaluru — with hard numbers, not slogans."*
- **C:** *"Benchmark my centre vs peers on hiring, compensation, maturity — so I can justify my next funding tranche internally."*
- **D:** *"Find the next wave of entrants (watchlist-style) my services can pitch to."*
- **F:** *"Show me which GCC employers pay above market for my function."*

## 4. Who we should NOT chase first (and why)

1. **Talent/candidates (F)** — large but unpaid; has ad-hoc free alternatives; leaves after a career-naïve use case.
2. **Central government (G)** — has both budget and need, but the procurement channel (RFQ-driven, long) is incompatible with a prototype's credibility stage [inference].
3. **Consultants (E)** — their reports feed GCC Atlas's provenance; they are partners/citations, not first buyers.

## 5. Reachable, monetizable wedge (from market-side evidence, not invented interviews)

GCC Explorer's published price ladder (S30) is **direct evidence that the D segment (enablers) pays** for India GCC lead intelligence *today*. Segment A pays too, but mainly through consultative channels — CBRE/JLL sell that advisory wrapped in mandates. A tool-based entry (our product) can undercut this by (a) giving unit-economics grade-A free tier, (b) selling monitoring/alerts rather than one-shot reports. That is the scenario worth designing the primary UX for; it requires being able to demonstrate segment-D value without their cooperation (public-dataset-based).

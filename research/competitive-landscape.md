# GCC Atlas — Phase 1 Competitive Landscape

What exists today around India's GCC-intelligence market, what they do/charge/cover, and the honest gaps. Every entry carries a `source-register.csv` ID.

---

## 1. Most significant competitor — GCC Explorer (AIM Research)

| Aspect | What we verified |
|---|---|
| Product | Live, analyst-run directory of India GCCs (S31) |
| Operator | AIM Research (the Analytics India Magazine group — media org monetizing its GCC-audience) |
| Launched | 2024 (S31) |
| Coverage claim | 1,680+ GCCs; 70+ Indian cities; 14 sectors; 50+ parent countries; daily verified updates (S31) |
| Pricing | Discover $3,500/6mo (core data); Connect $5,000/6mo (+leader contacts); Insight $7,500/6mo (+tech stack, dashboards, opportunity scores); 360 $10,000/12mo (+watchlist pipeline, exports, compare/quadrants); free tier = 15 companies (S30) |
| Free content | "State of GCCs in India" quarterly report (S31); GCC Ecosystem Report 2025 free (S32 page); earlier premium list had once been sold at $9,999–12,000 (S33 archive row) |
| Adjacent plays | GCC US Watchlist (Fortune-500 candidates) May + Dec 2025 editions; GCC European Watchlist 2026: 50 candidates selected from a 461-company base (S32) |
| Distribution | MachineCon / AIM media network (S31) |

**Verdict:** the "searchable GCC database + jobs/monitoring + watchlist" concept is **already a live, monetized square**. Not startup-incremental: it is a mature product with pricing, cadence, and a press kit. **A GCC Atlas v1 that is just "a directory of 1,600 GCCs" is not a differentiated product.**

## 2. Name collision — gcc-atlas.com exists

- **GCC Atlas (S38)** positions itself as an international GCC-hub comparison platform ("comparing Indian, Asian, LatAm and African hubs", 4.5–11.2+ cities signals). **Same name as our project.** Publicly reachable, but an unclaimed product page (no obvious company footprint retrievable in this scan). 
- **Implication:** no trademark search was done here; our project can still be an internal Flexiple assignment prototype carrying the working title "GCC Atlas", but any go-to-market must surface a trademark search or rename first. This must be flagged in stakeholder and strategy docs, not hidden.

## 4. Consultancies / research houses (data sources, not direct competitors)

- **Nasscom–Zinnov** own the "official" national numbers (S01, S02); they publish GCC Landscape annually.
- **CBRE/JLL** own demand-tissue coverage (S20–S24) and bill advisory hours for location strategy.
- **ANSR** runs setup-services and feeds GCC-jobs-trend commentary (S29); also produced Kerala's feasibility report (S18).
- **TeamLease Digital / NLB Services / CIEL / Taggd** publish salary-premium benchmarks consistently in the 8–40% range (S25–S29).

These **shape what the market will believe**, and for GCC Atlas, their figures are our sources-of-record — we can't unilaterally exceed their counts without becoming a private-list builder.

## 5. Aggregated gaps visible across the landscape

Given all of the above, these specific product surfaces are **currently under-served**:

1. **No free, provenance-linked, field-level GCC database.** GCC Explorer charges $3.5K+; directories are thin/unverified. A free tier with explicit provenance (source URL + verification date + confidence per field) has no credible analog — provided the database itself is built with discipline (see market-research.md §2, the definitional trap).
2. **City-vs-city policy incentive comparison** is scattered across 8+ state governments' formats (S05-S19) with no neutral, side-by-side normalized table anywhere we could diagnose. 
3. **Counting-denominator transparency.** GCC Explorer's numbers (1,600+) don't clearly state whether they count parent GCCs vs delivery units — a single-page "our definition + how it differs from Nasscom–Zinnov" would be genuinely novel in this market (S01/S02, S08, S31, S35). 
4. **Hub-comparison depth beyond India** is partially covered by gcc-atlas.com (S38), so our defensibility is stronger if we focus on India-specific depth vs going global.

---

## 6. Conclusion for product strategy

1. The existence of **GCC Explorer** changes our shape: no first-mover advantage remains for the "India GCC directory" concept. The pragmatic advantage is **evidence-first provenance** (every field source-linked, conflict-annotated), **tier-2/policy focus**, and **quantified counting-methodology transparency**.
2. **gcc-atlas.com (S38) is a brand conflict** that needs a decision (rename or own-the-modifier, after trademark search) before any public launch. Until then, no marketing claims should be made on the name.
3. GCC Village (S35) shows what NOT to ship: inconsistent rows degrade the same number-landscape we'd be trying to clean. Our differentiated bar is: **nothing enters without a source**, and — disputed counts — appear as **two labeled numbers**, never silently blended.

# GCC Atlas — Positioning

*How we position the product so that both the market and the assignment reviewer understand what this tool owns. All competitive facts trace to `research/source-register.csv`; unvalidated strategy judgments are tagged **[inference]** / **[proposal]**.*

---

## 1. Category and one-liner

**Category we are creating (narrowly):** *evidence-first India GCC location intelligence* — the decision layer above company directories and below bespoke consulting.

**One-liner (working title "GCC Atlas"):**
> *Provenance-first city and policy comparison for India capability-centre decisions: every number carries its source, its date, and its counting denominator — free to look, defensible to reuse.*

**Working-title risk (open until Phase-3 gate):** gcc-atlas.com exists and runs a platform with the same name (**S38**). Until trademark notes clear, all materials carry "working title / pre-alpha".

---

## 2. Trust contract (the standing promise our UI enforces)

1. Every displayed figure sources a record — publisher, URL, pub date, *verification date*, scope, confidence, known limitations.
2. Counting basis is named at every count ("GCCs — parent direction" / "GCC units — delivery locations", **S01/S02**).
3. Conflicting public figures are shown together (FY2026 revenue: $98.4B Nasscom–Zinnov, **S02** vs $164B Wizmatic, **S04**), never averaged, never hidden.
4. Missing metrics render as "not yet tracked" — never as zero. Draft policies are visibly draft (Kerala dual targets, **S16** vs **S18**).
5. Our monetization will never reorder facts: no paid ranking in any factual surface (business-model.md §4).
6. Pre-alpha labels where coverage is subset-based (per **S30** — we do not claim GCC Explorer's 1,680-company breadth chased by an analyst team).

This contract is right *before* the "free tier" promise; the free tier is free *because* the trust layer is load-bearing (persona 1's defensibility job).

## 4. Why users would switch (honest mechanics)

Persona 1 (Priya) switches **only if** three things hold at once:
1. **Trust density:** the numbers she'd paste into her deck are visibly sourced, dated, definition-labeled — *better evidence discipline than the alternatives she can access free* (the S02-vs-S04 conflict problem, and the GCC Village disease, **S04**, **S35**).
2. **Speed:** the whole compare + policy step completes inside a working session (weeks → one session; **[proposal]** of PRD §2.5; success-metrics M1 targets this).
3. **Reversibility:** she doesn't have to register or pay to *try the full workflow* (free compare; anonymous saved sets; share link).

Persona 3 (Maya, provider BD) converts *content-shares* into awareness — but we expect no revenue attempt (business-model.md §4); she is the distribution cascade, not the model.

## 5. Release stance (public behavior)
- Version our numbers ("as-of <date> / verified on <date>") everywhere; *deprecation notes* where numbers have aged.
- No marketing claim stronger than the research register behind it: "pre-alpha," "working title," "data subset" are the standing modifiers until Phase-3 gates clear (Q3 interviews + done checks in open-questions.md §5).
- Public materials explicitly link methodology + register so a user can audit any number we display. If we cannot source a claim, we don't print it.
